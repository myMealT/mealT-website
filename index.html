FAQ

Questions

How accurate is the photo logging?

It's an AI estimate. You see every item it found and can adjust portions or fix an item before anything is logged.

Who can see my meals?

Only you, unless you choose to share a meal with friends or a group. Shared posts disappear after 24 hours.

Does it work with Apple Health and Health Connect?

Yes. With your permission my MealT reads weight, steps and activity so your trends and calorie balance stay accurate.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Need help? Contact us.
